Core Recommendation Engine

The Mathematical Fairness Formula

ClassSwap never relies on random assignments or arbitrary guesswork. Every recommendation is scored through our multi-factor formula:

Final Score = (0.5 × Subject Match) + (0.3 × Fairness Score) + (0.2 × Department Match)
50%
Subject Match: 1.0 if qualified for the exact subject; 0.5 if faculty teaches a subject in the same discipline; 0.0 otherwise.
30%
Monthly Fairness Score: Dynamically normalized based on substitutions covered this month. Faculty with fewer substitutions receive higher priority to distribute the load evenly.
20%
Department Match: 1.0 if faculty belongs to the same department as the class subject; 0.0 otherwise.
