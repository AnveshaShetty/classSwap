The Workflow

How ClassSwap Works

From absence to covered class in four automated steps.

1

Faculty Marks Absence

Faculty selects date and period, submits absence reason. Admin is immediately notified.

2

AI Ranks Substitutes

Engine scores available faculty by subject match, workload fairness, and department alignment.

3

Free Room Allocated

System identifies genuinely free classrooms with sufficient capacity for the specific period.

✓

Class Continues

Substitute accepts, date-specific override is saved, and no class hour is wasted.
